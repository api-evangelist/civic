In the last six weeks the signal data layer became callable. PredictLeads exposes every dataset as a tool at mcp.predictleads.com, Coresignal shipped an MCP server with OAuth on August 4 and moved its company dataset to daily delivery on September 10, Demandbase lists native MCP support for enrichment and intent, and Clay puts its data providers and workflows behind an MCP server.
