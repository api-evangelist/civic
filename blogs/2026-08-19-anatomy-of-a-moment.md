Between a signal firing and a relevant touch landing there are six stages: signal, identity, score, Play, bounds, record. Each one is a place teams silently fail, and doing all six by hand, in minutes, is a staffing problem disguised as a discipline problem. Part 2 of 3 on personalization: the mechanics.
