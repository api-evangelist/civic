Dreamforce and UNBOUND are showing what agents can do inside Salesforce and HubSpot. Most growth teams I talk to do not work inside one platform: buyer activity moves across the website, product, CRM, Slack, outbound, and the data warehouse. The practical question is who is allowed to act across those systems, and where you can review the decisions and actions across the workflow.
