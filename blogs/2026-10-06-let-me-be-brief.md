Briefs, by Bryn is open as a Public Preview. Name a company and inside a minute Bryn hands you a page about it: who it is and whether it looks like a buyer, a score out of 100, the tools it runs, the people who would sign off, and the first follow-up Bryn would run. The last section is the receipt: the Brief was built by one of Bryn's own rules, and it prints the clock of every step.
