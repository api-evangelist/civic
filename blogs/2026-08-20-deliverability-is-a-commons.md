Email deliverability is a commons: every over-sender degrades the channel for everyone, including themselves. Google and Yahoo enforce a 0.3% spam-rate ceiling, most fully autonomous AI SDR pilots get pulled inside 90 days, and warmup tricks do not fix a grazing problem. A usage cap protects the budget.
